Mine it, dump it in the buyback, get paid on the spot. The math is already done and the rate is already fair. Hauling is for people without a corp.

Your goblin career

// the number climbs, the shelf fills
APPLY A real front door

No more shouting into local. A structured, always open application that a recruiter actually reviews, then hands you a one time claim code. Clean funnel, zero spam.

PORTAL Your career page

One login, all your alts nested under it. A haul number that climbs as you mine, a trophy case that fills, days in corp, your exact share of every op. Open the portal →

ACADEMY Learn to fly right

Six modules with real certification quizzes: the survival doctrine that ends the 3 day Hulk, how an op runs, how payday works. Pass them and earn your first medal on the spot.

LEDGER Fair, logged, provable

The Fleet Drop Ledger values every drop live and pays your exact proportional slice. The ledger saw you drop it, so nobody argues at the end. Fleet leads run the whole op from one screen.

ESI Tie in your real mining

Attach your EVE character on CCP's own login (we never see your password) and the portal pulls your true in game mining ledger, so everything you mine, solo or in fleet, counts toward leaderboards and events.

MEDALS The Wall of Goblins

Real honors that sit on your character forever. First Haul, The Provider, One of Us Now, the rare Cornerstone. The locked ones show you exactly how to earn them.
