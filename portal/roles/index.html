ROLES & PERMISSIONS

CORPORATION DOCTRINE · v1, PROPOSED · comments open to every member
This is corp doctrine. Log in and it opens.
The corp is big enough that leadership needs more hands, and handing out power in EVE deserves paperwork. This page is the paperwork. It lists every corporation role the game offers, what each one truly permits, what we expect from anyone holding it, and the title structure we propose to run. Read the parts that concern you, argue with the parts you disagree with, and know that the structure is deliberate: narrow grants, clear mandates, and an audit trail.

How the system works

Five mechanics explain almost everything below.
▸Roles vs titles roles are permissions, titles are named bundles of them

A role is a single game permission. A title bundles roles under a name and is assigned by the CEO or a Director. We assign titles, never loose roles: one place to audit, one thing to revoke, and your title on the member list says what you do.

▸Grantable roles the ability to give a role without holding it

A grantable role lets its holder assign that role to others without gaining the permission themselves. Director is never grantable and only the CEO assigns Directors. Our policy keeps grantables rare and deliberate.

▸Scoping hangar and container access comes per location: HQ, your base, or elsewhere

Hangar and container roles exist in three location flavors: at headquarters, at your assigned base (set by a Director), and at other offices. That is the precision tool: access exactly where a job needs it and nowhere else.

▸Divisions seven wallets and seven hangars, each with its own access

The corp has seven wallet divisions (division one is the Master Wallet) and seven hangar divisions, each nameable and each with separate query, take and container permissions. Money and materials are compartmented on purpose: a role opens one drawer, not the cabinet.

▸The 24 hour rule roles stick for a day after they are dropped

A member holding roles cannot leave into another player corporation or be removed until the roles are dropped and 24 hours pass. Every grant is therefore a grant for today and for a full day after anyone changes their mind. Access is sized with that in mind, and it is nothing personal: it is arithmetic.

Related: removing a member at all is Director tier by game design (Personnel Manager cannot kick). That is why the structure below keeps more than one person who can start that clock.

General roles

CUSTODY holds corp assets · SPEND can commit corp resources · INTEL sees sensitive information · SAFE is safe.
▸Director every other role implicitly, plus kicks, bases, corp detailscustody
Mechanics

Carries every other role and its grantable form, edits corporation details, views shareholders, assigns member bases, removes members. Assigned only by the CEO.

Responsibilities

Full fiduciary trust. A Director can do anything the corp can do, so the list stays as short as the corp can survive.

Assignment

CEO retained. Appointments are announced, never quiet.

▸Accountant all wallet journals and bills; can also take from corp deliveriescustody
Mechanics

Views bills plus every wallet division's journal and transaction log. Separately, and less obviously, it is one of the roles that can remove items from the Corporation Deliveries hangar. It is not a read only role.

Responsibilities

Treasury oversight, when delegated at all.

Assignment

CEO retained.

▸Junior Accountant wallet balances and bills, not the journalsintel
Mechanics

Sees division balances and corp bills. Journals and transaction logs stay closed (the transaction log opens only in combination with Trader).

Assignment

CEO retained. Balances still tell a story.

▸Auditor the role change log: who granted what, whensafe
Mechanics

Reads the record of role changes: date, action, initiator. Touches nothing.

Responsibilities

The watchdog. Reviews the log on a cadence and flags anything unexplained. This role exists so trust is verified, not assumed.

Assignment

Carried by the WATCHDOG title.

▸Personnel Manager accepts applications, awards medals; cannot kickspend
Mechanics

Accepts and rejects membership applications and awards existing decorations. Creating decorations stays with leadership, and each award costs the corp 5 million ISK, permanently: awards cannot be revoked.

Responsibilities

The door. Every acceptance follows the vetting flow, no exceptions for friends, and medal awards follow the honors list.

Assignment

Carried by the RECRUITER title.

▸Communications Officer bulletins, corp calendar, channel MOTD, corp bookmarksspend
Mechanics

Adds and edits corp bulletins and calendar events, edits the corp channel message of the day and corp bookmark folders.

Responsibilities

The voice. Ops on the calendar, the MOTD current, announcements clean.

Assignment

Carried by the HERALD title.

▸Diplomat edits corp standings toward other entitiesspend
Mechanics

Sets and edits the corporation's standings list. Standings drive who reads as friendly on every member's overview, so an error here is everyone's error.

Responsibilities

Alliance and neighbor relations. Standings changes are announced when they happen.

Assignment

Carried by the ENVOY title.

▸Fitting Manager maintains the corp's saved fittingssafe

Adds, edits and removes corporation ship fittings.

Responsibilities

Doctrine fits stay current and named clearly.

Assignment

Carried by the HERALD title.

▸Skill Plan Manager manages corporation skill planssafe

Creates and maintains the corp's shared skill plans.

Keeps the Academy's in game skill plans matched to what we actually fly.

Assignment

Carried by the HERALD title.

▸Project Manager creates and closes Corporation Projectsspend
Mechanics

Creates, closes and deletes Corporation Projects. A project that pays ISK additionally requires access to a corp wallet division, which is why this role rides with one capped payout wallet and nothing else.

Responsibilities

Turns the corp's needs into posted, paid objectives: mine this ore, haul this route, kill these rats. The works plan decides what; projects make it happen.

Assignment

Carried by the PROJECT LEAD title.

▸Brand Manager creates and applies structure SKINsspend

Creates SKINR designs and applies them to the corp's Upwell structures, consuming SKINR materials.

Assignment

CEO retained. The Athanor's look is a leadership vice.

▸Contract Manager creates contracts in the corp's namecustody
Mechanics

Creates corporation contracts. To actually move anything it needs a wallet division and hangar take access, so it is granted as a package into one capped wallet and one intake hangar, never broadly.

Responsibilities

Runs buyback intake: accepts ore contracts at desk quotes, settles from the desk float, moves ore into the Keep.

Assignment

Carried by the DESK OFFICER title.

▸Config Equipment configures audit log containersintel

Assignment

Granted alongside a hangar mandate when needed.

▸Starbase roles legacy POS control: config, defense, fuelcustody
Mechanics

Config Starbase Equipment anchors, unanchors and configures legacy starbases. Starbase Defense Operator fires their weapons. Starbase Fuel Technician views and refuels them. We operate Upwell structures, not starbases.

Not granted. Legacy.

Station services

▸Station Manager Upwell structure settings: docking, services, profilescustody
Mechanics

Edits a corporation's Upwell structure configuration: docking rights, service and slot availability, structure profiles.

Assignment

Inert here, deliberately. Our structures are held by a separate entity, so no role inside this corp can touch the station. That is not an oversight, it is the blast wall: whatever happens to any role in this corp, the Athanor keeps standing and the doors keep working.

▸Security Officer views member personal hangars at corp officesintel
Mechanics

Views members' personal hangars at stations where the corp holds an office and can place items into them, but never remove.

Assignment

CEO retained. Surveillance of members is not delegated.

▸Factory Manager runs corp industry jobs from corp assetsspend
Mechanics

Installs, cancels and delivers corp manufacturing and research jobs using corporation assets. Pairs with the rent slot roles and hangar access on the industry division to function.

Responsibilities

The print queue: runs posted builds from the industry hangar, delivers product back, touches nothing outside the mandate.

Assignment

Carried by the FOREMAN title.

▸Rent Office / Rent Factory Slot / Rent Research Slot rents offices and industry slots on the corp's accountspend
Mechanics

The rent roles commit corp money. Rent Office in particular requires no wallet role at all to spend, which is why it is never in a title. The slot roles ride with FOREMAN because industry does not run without them.

Assignment

Slots with FOREMAN. Offices CEO retained.

▸Trader corp market orders and the deliveries hangarcustody
Mechanics

Creates market orders in the corp's name, buys with corp funds when paired with wallet access, and holds full access to the Corporation Deliveries hangar where every corp market purchase and sale lands.

Assignment

CEO retained.

Division roles

▸Account Take 1 to 7 withdraw and transfer from that wallet divisioncustody
Mechanics

Each grant opens exactly one wallet division for withdrawals and transfers. Division one is the Master Wallet and is CEO retained without exception. Working divisions are kept topped to their purpose, not pooled.

Assignment

Division two with DESK OFFICER, division five with PROJECT LEAD. Others CEO retained.

▸Hangar Query 1 to 7 view one hangar division at one location scopeintel
Mechanics

Sight without touch, per division, per location scope. The default first grant for any hangar mandate.

▸Hangar Take 1 to 7 take from one hangar division at one location scopecustody
Mechanics

Removal rights, per division, per location. Granted only where a mandate needs its hands: intake for the desk, work in progress for industry.

▸Container Take 1 to 7 remove whole containers from a divisioncustody
Mechanics

Lifts entire containers out of a hangar division, contents and all. Opening a container's contents needs the matching hangar take.

Assignment

CEO retained.

Proposed titles

The bundles we intend to run. Each is one checkbox set, one mandate, one line on the member list.
▸Deputy Director Director · exactly one, appointment announced corp wide

Continuity. A corp this size cannot depend on one person's login for titles, removals, base assignments or division changes. The Deputy carries the full Director role, which is why there is exactly one, why the appointment is public, and why the seat is the most carefully chosen line on this page. No mechanic reduces Director trust; the structure compensates instead: the station sits behind the blast wall and the corp's books are kept and reconciled outside the game.

▸Recruiter Personnel Manager

Works the door: vets and accepts applicants through the standing process, awards medals from the honors list. The corp grows through this title.

▸Herald Communications Officer + Fitting Manager + Skill Plan Manager

The corp's voice and library: calendar, MOTD, bulletins, doctrine fits, Academy skill plans. Wide responsibility, zero asset surface, which is exactly the point.

▸Envoy Diplomat

Standings and neighbor relations, announced as they change.

▸Desk Officer Contract Manager + one capped wallet + intake hangar at HQ

Runs buyback intake: accepts ore contracts at desk quotes, settles from the desk float, moves ore onward. Exposure is one float and one hangar, deliberately.

▸Foreman Factory Manager + rent slots + industry hangar at HQ

Runs the print queue: posted builds from the industry hangar, blueprints arriving by contract, product delivered back. Sees the mineral stock, takes only work in progress.

▸Project Lead Project Manager + one capped payout wallet

Posts paid Corporation Projects off the corp's actual needs: what to mine, what to haul, what to clear. The machine says what it wants; this title puts ISK behind it.

▸Watchdog Auditor

Reads the role change log on a cadence and asks the questions out loud. Trust, verified.

This is v1 and it is not scripture. If a mandate is missing, a grant is too tight, or you want a seat: say so. The suggestion box on the portal takes it signed or anonymous, or raise it with any officer in Discord. Doctrine gets amended by argument, not by silence.
