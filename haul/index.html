PHEW is at war with nobody. It has not slowed them down at all.

What they do instead: on 28 July, at 07:31 and 07:34, one pilot from a PHEW member corp killed two of our mining ships three minutes apart, solo, in a wormhole. No war, no CONCORD, no consequences. Roughly 556 million ISK in three minutes.

The Obelisk is the one worth sitting with. Fifty-nine attackers in Uedama is not bad luck and it is not something a better pilot survives. That is an industry with a shift schedule, and the only defences that work against it are the ones you apply before undocking: cargo value, timing, and route.

Everything else on your board is small, which is the actual compliment here. Your crew does not lose freight often. When you do, it is at a famous chokepoint or deep in null, and both of those are route decisions rather than piloting ones.

05 THE BLIND SPOT, STATED PLAINLY

Our tripwire watches the corridor and three regions, and it is good at that. It did not catch this morning's two kills, because they happened in a wormhole, and a wormhole is in none of those lists. Nobody got a warning. We are telling you that rather than quietly fixing it, because you route through places our sensors do not cover and you should know exactly where the edge of our vision is.

Silence from us is not the same as safety. It means nothing died where we were looking. We are moving to alerting on any alliance loss anywhere, not just inside the watched systems.

06 WHAT WE ASK, AND WHAT YOU GET

  1. Tell us before the expensive runs. Not permission, warning. An escort that knows the route exists is worth more than one scrambled after a gate goes red.
  2. Post your losses, even the embarrassing ones. Every hull on the board becomes a file, and a file is what stops the second one.
  3. Use the road rules above. Most of what killed freight this year was a route choice made before undock.
  4. What you get back: corridor danger read live on the map, named files on everyone working this ground, an escort wing standing up now, and a straight answer whenever you ask whether the road is clear. If we do not know, we will say we do not know.
