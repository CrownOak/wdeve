01 THE HEADLINE

PHEW is at war with nobody. It has not slowed them down at all.

We scanned six thousand war declarations, covering December 2025 to this week, against PURPLE HELMETED WARRIORS [PHEW] and all eighteen of their member corps. They hold no active wars. Four in seven months, every one closed by 13 July:

For an alliance with 224,000 lifetime kills and 723 members, that is close to noise. The conclusion is not that they are harmless. It is that highsec war is not the tool they use, so the war list tells you nothing about your real risk.

What they do instead: on 28 July at 07:31 and 07:34, one pilot from a PHEW member corp killed two of our mining ships three minutes apart, solo, in a wormhole. No war, no CONCORD, no consequences. About 556 million ISK in three minutes.

The number that matters is the last one. Derelik has no nullsec at all, and it is 44% lowsec. This is not a safe region with a rough edge; it is a region that is nearly half rough, with a highsec pocket we happen to live in. That single fact explains the corridor, the escorts, and most of this document.

The Obelisk is the one worth sitting with. Fifty-nine attackers in Uedama is not bad luck and it is not something a better pilot survives. That is an industry with a shift schedule, and the only defences that work are applied before undock: cargo value, timing, route.

Everything else on your board is small, which is the real compliment. Your crew does not lose freight often. When you do it is at a famous chokepoint or deep in null, and both of those are route decisions rather than piloting ones.

09 THE BLIND SPOT, STATED PLAINLY

Our tripwire watches the corridor and three regions and it is good at that. It did not catch this morning's two kills, because they happened in a wormhole, and a wormhole is in none of those lists. Nobody got a warning. We are telling you rather than quietly fixing it, because you route through places our sensors do not cover and you should know exactly where the edge of our vision sits.

Silence from us is not the same as safety. It means nothing died where we were looking. We are moving to alerting on any alliance loss anywhere, not only inside the watched systems.

10 THE DEAL

  1. Tell us before the expensive runs. Not permission, warning. An escort that knows the route exists is worth more than one scrambled after a gate goes red.
  2. Post your losses, even the embarrassing ones. Every hull on the board becomes a file, and a file is what stops the second one.
  3. Use the road rules. Most of what killed freight this year was a route choice made before undock.
  4. What you get back: corridor danger read live on the map, named files on everyone working this ground, an escort wing standing up now, and a straight answer whenever you ask whether the road is clear. If we do not know, we will say we do not know.
Golden Duck Company is what happens without the fourth item. That is the only sales pitch in this document and we would rather you never need it.
