How to use: pick a mode. Ore to Materials: in game select your ore (Ctrl+A or Ctrl+click), copy with Ctrl+C, paste here. Ore Prices: paste the same ore and see what the load is worth raw at Jita, Amarr, Dodixie, Rens, and Hek, which hub actually pays, and the full price board for every rock in the game. Materials to Ore: paste the minerals you need (like Tritanium 5000000) and get how much ore to mine for them, then hit Calculate. Project Tracker (corp password): a shared Keep plus build queue that shows the shortfall, what to mine, and what to buy to fulfill the whole queue.
