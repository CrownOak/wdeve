Why your cargo tooltip says one number and the desk says another

The number in your cargo tooltip is real. It is also not a buyer. It is the game's polite guess at what the minerals inside your rocks might fetch at a trade hub, if the rocks were already minerals, and the minerals were already twenty jumps away, and somebody there wanted all of them today.

Between your cargo hold and that number stands the entire supply chain: reprocessing (the refinery keeps a cut, physics does not negotiate), hauling (a freighter full of ore is just a piñata with an itinerary), hub fees and taxes, market skills, and time (sell orders are not money, they are homework with a spread).

The buyback skips all of it. You drop ore at the home station, ISK appears, you are back in the belt before your wallet stops blinking. We do the refining, the hauling, the market grind, and the getting shot at. That service is the product.

The three doors

You never have to use the desk. Three doors, always open. Contract it to the corp and get paid now. Haul it to a hub yourself and squeeze the top price out of it, we will wave at the gate. Or refine it and build things, the industry program will take every mineral you can make. Pick a different door every day. Nobody keeps score, the rocks certainly do not.

Want to compare doors first? The calculator prices your load at every hub. The desk quotes what we pay. Both numbers are honest, they are just answering different questions: what your ore might be worth somewhere, versus what it is worth right here, right now, with zero effort attached.
